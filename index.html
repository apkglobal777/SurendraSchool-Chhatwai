Progressive Stages of Learning

Affiliated to CBSE (10+2) NEP 2020 Compliant

Foundational Stage

Primary Wing (Nursery - V)

Sensory exploration, phonetics mastery, activity-centered numeracy, mental calculation routines, and foundational Hindi and English communicative flair.

Key Focus:
  • Montessori & Playway method
  • Motor skills & rhythm arts
02
Preparatory Stage

Middle Wing (Grades VI - VIII)

Introduction to formal physics, chemistry, biology labs, computational programming, Sanskrit language roots, and scientific hypothesis testing.

Key Focus:
  • Experiential STEM modules
  • Debate, oratory, drama societies
03
Secondary Stage

Secondary Wing (Grades IX - X)

Intensive CBSE Board preparation with analytical assignments, pre-board simulations, remedial clinical tutoring, and career interest profiling.

Key Focus:
  • CBSE Board Exam Mastery
  • NTSE & Olympiad training
04
Senior Stage

Senior Sec. (Grades XI - XII)

Specialized Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, and Humanities streams with synchronized coaching modules for JEE Main/Advanced, NEET, and CUET.

Key Focus:
  • Integrated Entrance Mentorship
  • Dedicated Advanced Research Labs

Admission Roadmap & Criteria

We welcome earnest learners eager to push their boundaries. Our admissions process is transparent, merits-focused, and supportive of every child's individual background.

1

Step 1: Registration & Inquiry

Submit the online form below or obtain the physical registration packet from the Chhatwai school campus reception.

2

Step 2: Campus Interaction & Assessment

Informal conversational assessment for Pre-Primary; baseline diagnostic written evaluation for Grade 1 upwards.

3

Step 3: Document Verification

Submission of Birth Certificate, previous school Transfer Certificate (TC), Aadhaar copies, and academic transcripts.

4

Step 4: Formal Enrolment & Induction

Fee payment receipt, issue of student ID, uniform allotment, bus route assignment, and welcome orientation.
